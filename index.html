Description

John Higgins Auctioneer in association with Sherry Fitzgerald Hanley are delighted to present this exciting opportunity to acquire a truly immaculate 4 Bedroom detached Bungalow, in turn-key condition throughout, set on beautiful manicured gardens within a short stroll to the Marian village of Knock. Located just 1.5km from the picturesque village, this property is situated in the pleasant and scenic rural area of Ballyhowley with stunning uninterrupted views of the rolling countryside.

The bungalow was originally constructed in 1978 and has been meticulously maintained and upgraded by the current owners regardless of cost. Accommodation extends to c. 1,140 sq.ft and includes Entrance Hallway, Sitting Room, Kitchen with open plan living area, Dining Room, 4 Bedrooms (1 of which is En-suite), Utility and recently refurbished family Bathroom. The property enjoys an abundance of light from early morning to late evening.

Central heating is provided by a gas fired system ably assisted by an open fireplace and there are double glazed PVC windows throughout and services include connection to mains water and sewage. The House is set on an elevated 0.32 Acre Site and will impress immediately upon arrival with its beautiful gardens, shrubs, flower beds and will appeal to any garden enthusiast. A tarmac driveway along is also included to the front and to the rear there is further garden areas, detached garage/workshop along with a garden shed suited to a variety of domestic uses and a neatly presented patio area finished with high quality paving ideal for those summer evening barbeques.

The property as previously mentioned earlier enjoys extensive and panoramic views of the surrounding green countryside and has to be seen to be appreciated. This is a superb opportunity to acquire a beautiful detached residential property, ready for immediate occupation with no further outlay required.

Further enquiries or an appointment to view can be made by contacting the joint selling agents John Higgins Auctioneer on 094 9631372 or Sherry Fitzgerald Hanley Claremorris on 094 9371479.
